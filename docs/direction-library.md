# Direction Contract — Library Surface (The Antiquarian Catalogue)

## THESIS
The library is an antiquarian bookseller's catalogue: every book a numbered plate in a hairline double-rule frame with a typed specimen label. It refuses the app-store grid of variable-height cover thumbnails and the settings-menu density of upstream readers.

## OWN-WORLD
Paper ground `#F3EDE0`; ink `#26221B`; muted `#6E6350`; single oxblood accent `#8C3B22`; optional sage `#5B6B4F` for secondary state ink. Plates: 1px hairline frame at ink/25%, inner 1px rule inset 3px (letterpress dent), optional 4px ticket notch on interactive plates. Type: Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Special Elite typed microcopy. Ornaments: ✳ / ✦ at 12px as separators only.

## STORY
The reader understands at a glance: this is a collection, each book catalogued and equal. They believe the app treats their library with curatorial care. They do: pick a plate and listen.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Masthead: PALIMPSEST wordmark (Fraunces, letterspaced) left, hairline rule beneath, typed catalog line right ("№ {count} volumes · {date}"). Below: one uniform grid, 10 plates per row, each plate a 2:3 cropped cover, typed № label top-left, 3-line label beneath (title / author · progress / plate № · format). No sidebar, no filters, no sorting menus. Primary action: click a plate.
