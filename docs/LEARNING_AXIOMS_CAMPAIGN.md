# THE LEARNING AXIOMS CAMPAIGN — constitution (owner ruling 2026-11-26)

**The question:** learning *should* be easy — resources are infinite, access is
free, AI can explain anything. Yet it isn't. Why? And what would make it "easy
as shit to learn anything new"?

**The method:** derive clear, general axioms from first principles across
philosophy, psychology, contemplative traditions, fiction, and the hidden
pedagogies — then build the plan on the axioms. Meta first, concrete later.

**The stance:** nothing is off-limits. Mainstream science, esoteric traditions,
fiction (which holds truths not yet manifested), random wikis — all are evidence.
Every claim gets an epistemic label: [established] (replicated science),
[contested] (real debate), [tradition] (long practice, thin science), [fiction]
(truth unmanifested), [speculative] (our synthesis).

## The five lanes

L1 — PHILOSOPHY OF LEARNING: what is learning, understanding, knowledge?
  Plato's anamnesis (Meno), Aristotle's phronesis vs episteme, Dewey's
  experiential learning, Wittgenstein's meaning-is-use, Heidegger's
  readiness-to-hand, Gadamer's fusion of horizons, Confucius, the Upanishads,
  Krishnamurti (Freedom from the Known — learning as his central theme),
  Whitehead's Aims of Education, Illich's Deschooling Society.

L2 — THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE BLOCKED LEARNER: why abundance doesn't help.
  Self-determination theory (Deci & Ryan: autonomy/competence/relatedness),
  flow (Csikszentmihalyi), self-efficacy (Bandura), the affective filter
  (Krashen), identity ("I'm not a math person" — the self-fulfilling ceiling),
  Dweck's growth mindset (with honest replication caveats), the protégé
  effect, psychological safety, play, Polya's metacognition, the emotional
  cost of being bad at something (the #1 blocker?).

L3 — EASTERN MEDITATION & CONTEMPLATIVE TRADITIONS: attention as the
  trainable substrate of learning. Vipassana as attention training, Zen
  shoshin (beginner's mind), non-attachment to outcome (the fear-of-wrong
  unlock), the jhanas as flow, Krishnamurti's choiceless awareness, the
  Tao Te Ching on learning, Thich Nhat Hanh, the no-self and identity
  dissolution as the removal of the "I'm not good at this" blocker.
  The honest bridge: what meditation actually does to learning (the
  research) vs. what the traditions claim.

L4 — FICTION AS UNMANIFESTED TRUTH: stories are the original learning
  technology. Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance, Siddhartha,
  The Little Prince, Flowers for Algernon, Ender's Game, The Name of the
  Wind, Musashi's Book of Five Rings, Dune, Gödel Escher Bach, Borges,
  The Alchemist. The mentor archetype (Obi-Wan, Dumbledore, Mr. Miyagi,
  Oogway) as encoded teaching wisdom. Video games as the most effective
  learning environments ever built (Gee, Breath of the Wild, Outer Wilds
  as the purest learning game). What do stories know about learning that
  papers don't?

L5 — THE HIDDEN PEDAGOGIES: the deep esoteric shit. Frances Yates' The
  Art of Memory (the memory palace — real, proven, ancient), lectio divina
  (slow sacred reading), monastic study, oral tradition (Homeric memory),
  apprenticeship (learning by doing with a master), Sufi pedagogy, the
  Pythagorean community, speed-reading myths vs reality, the lost/forbidden
  teaching techniques, and honest epistemic labeling throughout.

## Synthesis (L6)

Combine into: (1) the AXIOMS — general, clear, load-bearing truths about why
learning is hard and what makes it easy; (2) the meta-model — how the axioms
fit together; (3) the map from each axiom to a concrete mechanism in Palimpsest
(the professor, the desk, the ledger, the loop). The axioms are the constitution
for everything we build next.

## Constraints

- Every axiom must trace to evidence in at least one lane, labeled.
- The synthesis must answer the founding question: WHY is learning hard
  despite infinite resources?
- Nothing is too esoteric; everything is labeled honestly.
